To You,

I know that in the grand narrative of your life, I was likely just a background character—a nameless face sitting in the opposite corner of the room, fading quietly into the crowd. But for me, you were the entire plot.

You are like the sun to my moon. You radiate light so effortlessly, complete and whole entirely on your own. But without you, I am not. I only ever existed in the warmth you cast across those desks, reflecting a light that was always yours.

You might never care for me, and maybe you never even knew my name, but every single instant of those quiet hours belonged to you. I wish I could have told you that seeing just one of your smiles felt like eternal happiness—as if, in a single second, I had found everything I could ever want in this world. You were the partner I dreamed of but never got to hold; the center of a universe you didn't even know you created.

I wish I had told you that while everyone else was listening to a lesson on diverged paths, I was standing at the hardest crossroad of my life: deciding whether to keep hiding in the safety of silence, or to walk across the room and hand you my heart.

Choosing to stay quiet was the easy path. But telling you all of this—standing before you and risking it all—that was the road not taken. And ever since that afternoon, it is the one declaration I wish I had given to you.
